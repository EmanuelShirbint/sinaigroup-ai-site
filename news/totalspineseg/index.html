SINAIGROUP AI CTO Co-Authors International Medical AI Research

Emanuel Shirbint, Co-Founder and CTO of SINAIGROUP AI LTD, is a co-author of the scientific paper “TotalSpineSeg: automated whole-spine vertebral segmentation with high robustness to labeling errors.”

The study addresses automated spinal segmentation and anatomical labeling in medical imaging, with a focus on robust deep learning methods.

Emanuel's research contributions included MRI data processing, 2D-to-3D preparation, nnU-Net experimentation, model training, and parameter optimization. This work was conducted as part of an international research collaboration associated with Sheba Medical Center, not as a SINAIGROUP AI company project.

The publication reflects scientific expertise relevant to the company's focus on advanced AI, data engineering, and governed analytical systems.
